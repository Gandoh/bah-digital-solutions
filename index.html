Good to know / 04

Before we
begin.

A few practical answers about working together.

Can we start with a small project?

Yes. A focused paid trial with a defined deliverable is a sensible way to assess fit before discussing larger work.

Do you work with clients outside Sierra Leone?

We work remotely and welcome international enquiries. Scope, communication, payment method and delivery terms are agreed before a project starts.

Are the portfolio examples real client projects?

No. They are independently created demonstrations using illustrative information, clearly identified so you can evaluate our approach without misleading claims.

What should I include in my enquiry?

Tell us the type of document, research or spreadsheet you need, your deadline and any existing examples or source materials. We can then discuss a practical scope.
